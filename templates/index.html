About Insider Threats

Insider threats are security risks that originate from within an organization. They typically involve:

  • Data exfiltration - Unauthorized copying or transferring of sensitive data
  • Sabotage - Deliberate disruption of systems or business operations
  • Fraud - Manipulation of systems for financial or personal gain
  • Intellectual property theft - Stealing trade secrets or proprietary information

This detection system uses machine learning to identify behavioral patterns associated with these threats.

How It Works

ML Analysis

Alert System
